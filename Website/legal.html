Terms of Service

1. What FlowShield is

FlowShield is a Windows desktop application that blocks applications you nominate during focus sessions you start. A free tier is available. A paid subscription ("Pro") unlocks additional features.

2. What the software does to your computer

This matters more than usual, so it is stated plainly: FlowShield closes running programs. When a focus session or a scheduled sleep window is active, any application on your block list is terminated, and any unsaved work in it may be lost. You choose what goes on that list.

FlowShield will never close operating-system processes, shells, or common development tools; these are excluded and the exclusion is not configurable. Even so, you are responsible for what you add to your block list. Do not add anything you cannot afford to have closed without warning.

3. Your subscription

  • Pro is billed monthly in advance at the price shown at checkout.
  • It renews automatically each month until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time; Pro features remain available until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Payment is processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Prices may change. We will tell you before a change affects you, and you may cancel instead of accepting it.

4. Your licence key

Your licence is for your own use. Sharing your key or the email address attached to your subscription lets other people unlock Pro, and doing so is a breach of these terms. We may deactivate a licence we reasonably believe is being shared or resold.

5. Availability

Activating and validating a licence requires our licence service to be reachable. We try to keep it up and make no promise that it always will be. FlowShield's blocking works offline once activated.

6. Liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, we are not liable for lost work, lost data, lost profits, or any indirect loss arising from using it — including from an application being closed. Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for death, personal injury, or fraud.

Where consumer law gives you rights that these terms cannot remove, those rights still apply.

7. Ending your access

You may stop using FlowShield at any time and uninstall it. We may suspend or end access if these terms are breached. If we end your access without cause, we will refund the unused part of the period you have paid for.

8. Changes

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Your email address, given at checkout, so we can send your licence key and contact you about your subscription.
  • Your licence key and subscription status, so we can tell whether your subscription is active.
  • Payment records held by Stripe. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and the last part of your card number; we never receive or store the full number.

What stays on your computer

Your block list, focus sessions, journal entries, momentum score and preferences are stored locally and encrypted with Windows DPAPI under your user account. None of it is uploaded. We do not know which applications you block, when you work, or what you write in your journal.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No cookies on the website beyond what a purchase requires.
  • We never sell or rent your information.

Who else is involved

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management.
  • Our hosting provider — runs the licence service.
  • Our email provider — delivers your licence key.

Each receives only what it needs to do that job.

How long we keep it

While your subscription is active, and afterwards only as long as tax and accounting rules require for the payment record.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Deleting your record cancels your subscription, since the record is what proves you have one. Write to the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Refund Policy

14-day refund

If FlowShield is not what you wanted, email us within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. You do not need to give a reason.

Renewals

Because the subscription renews automatically, a renewal can be charged for a month you did not mean to buy. If you contact us within 14 days of a renewal and have not used FlowShield in that period, we will refund it.

Cancelling

Cancel at any time from the billing portal linked in the app, or by emailing us. Cancelling stops future charges; Pro keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for.

Statutory rights
