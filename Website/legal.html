Terms of Service

1. What FlowShield is

FlowShield is a Windows desktop application that blocks applications you nominate during focus sessions you start. You can use every feature free for 7 days from the first time you open it. After that, FlowShield must be bought to keep using it. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

2. What the software does to your computer

This matters more than usual, so it is stated plainly: FlowShield closes running programs. During a focus session, Soft records the blocked application and shows a brief notice inside FlowShield, but leaves the application running. Firm and Sealed close it; Hard kill mode also makes Soft close it. During a scheduled sleep window, blocked applications are closed. Any unsaved work in an application FlowShield closes may be lost. You choose what goes on that list.

FlowShield will never close operating-system processes, shells, or common development tools; these are excluded and the exclusion is not configurable. Even so, you are responsible for what you add to your block list. Do not add anything you cannot afford to have closed without warning.

3. Your purchase

  • FlowShield costs a single payment at the price shown at checkout. Nothing renews and you will not be charged again.
  • The free trial lasts 7 days from the first launch. When it ends, the app locks until a licence is activated; nothing you saved is deleted.
  • Your purchase covers the current version and the updates we publish for it.
  • Payment is processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Prices may change for future buyers. A change never affects a purchase you have already made.

4. Your licence key

Your licence is for your own use. Sharing your key or the email address attached to your purchase lets other people activate FlowShield, and doing so is a breach of these terms. We may deactivate a licence we reasonably believe is being shared or resold.

5. Availability

Activating and validating a licence requires our licence service to be reachable. We try to keep it up and make no promise that it always will be. FlowShield's blocking works offline once activated.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Your email address, given at checkout, so we can send your licence key and contact you about your purchase.
  • Your licence key and purchase status, so we can tell whether your licence is active.
  • Payment records held by Stripe. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and the last part of your card number; we never receive or store the full number.

What stays on your computer

Your block list, focus sessions, journal entries, momentum score and preferences are stored locally and encrypted with Windows DPAPI under your user account. None of it is uploaded. We do not know which applications you block, when you work, or what you write in your journal.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No cookies on the website beyond what a purchase requires.
  • We never sell or rent your information.

Who else is involved

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Our hosting provider — runs the licence service.
  • Our email provider — delivers your licence key.

Each receives only what it needs to do that job.

How long we keep it

While your licence is active, and afterwards only as long as tax and accounting rules require for the payment record.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Deleting your record deactivates your licence, since the record is what proves you bought it. Write to the address below and we will respond within 30 days.
