Support

Help with FlowShield, and how to reach us.

Get help

If something is not working, the fastest way to get an answer is to send us FlowShield's log. Open FlowShield, go to Settings and click Open diagnostic log; it opens the log file the app keeps on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically — you choose whether to share it.

Email that file to [support email] with a short description of what happened and what you expected instead. The log lists the apps FlowShield closed, so skim it first if that is not something you want to share.

Before you write

  • Check the changelog — the fix may already be in a newer version.
  • Make sure you are on the latest release; FlowShield updates itself.
  • If a blocked app was not closed, confirm it is on your blocklist and that the sprint used Firm or Sealed (Soft only shows a notice unless Hard kill mode is on).

Purchases and refunds

FlowShield is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and nothing renews, so there is nothing to cancel. Pricing and the free trial are on the pricing section of the home page, and the refund policy is in the refund policy.
