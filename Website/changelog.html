Changelog

What has changed in FlowShield, newest first.

Unreleased

  • Study templates: Homework evening, Exam prep and Light study, one click on Today, and your own.
  • Schedules: a template can start by itself on the days and time you pick, with a heads-up five minutes before that names the open apps it will close, and Skip today.
  • A start missed while the PC was asleep or FlowShield was closed is offered for 30 minutes, never started.
  • Sleep Blocking is now the Schedule page; the nightly window is unchanged, at the bottom.
  • The Soft notice has a Close button, waits a few seconds before Allow 5 minutes, and shows what you planned.
  • The summary card and History show how many times you turned back from a blocked app.
  • Right-click FlowShield on the taskbar to start a sprint or any template.
  • Delete everything also clears FlowShield's taskbar Jump List.
  • The terms and privacy policy are updated to version 1.1 to cover schedules, Soft's Close button and the taskbar list, so FlowShield asks you to agree again once.
  • Fixed: a sprint that runs out while your PC is asleep is no longer recorded as longer than the sprint itself, so your focus minutes, goal and History stay right.
  • Fixed: inside your nightly sleep window, the break offer and the timer's caption now say the sleep shield is still up, instead of promising the shield is down.
  • Fixed: the five-minutes-left notification no longer promises the shield comes down inside your sleep window, and long captions under the timer now wrap inside the ring instead of running across it.

1.0.9 23 September 2026

  • A History page: this week's hours focused, sprints completed and distractions caught, plus a 30-day heatmap of focus minutes.
  • Breaks between sprints, and a study cycle that runs two, three or four sprints back to back with a break in between.
  • Blocklist profiles: keep a School list and a Gaming break list separately, and switch between them instead of editing one list every time.
  • Start a sprint from the tray icon or the keyboard — Shift+1/2/3 picks a shield and starts, Ctrl+1–5 moves between pages.
  • Soft now shows a full-screen notice on the blocked app's own screen, with Back to work and Allow 5 minutes, instead of a toast easy to miss behind it.
  • A quieter trial: only the sidebar tier badge, one notice on day 6, and the lock screen after day 7.
  • Settings → Your data: exactly what FlowShield stores and what leaves the PC, with buttons to export it or delete everything.
  • Honest waiting on Activate: the app now says when the licence server is waking up, and keeps retrying instead of treating a slow server as a rejected key.
  • Settings → your devices: see every PC a licence is active on, and release one you no longer use.
  • A "Lost your key?" form on the support page that re-sends it to your checkout email.
  • Appearance: System / Dark / Light in Settings; a visible keyboard focus ring; every control named for screen readers.
  • Six fixes to the sprint engine and blocker: the streak and daily goal now read correctly, and the blocker no longer enforces less than it promises.
  • Fixed: a background licence check could collide with the UI and lose a just-finished sprint instead of saving it.
  • Fixed: checkout was failing for every buyer.
  • The app's pages now adapt to any window size, and the nav icons no longer clip.
  • Settings is grouped into six sections you can jump between.
  • The timer ring shows the time left: it starts full and drains smoothly as the sprint or break runs. With Windows' animation effects off it steps once a second.
  • Find an app lists only apps installed on your PC, each with its own icon, and leaves out updaters, installers, runtimes and other entries that aren't apps.
  • Fixed: the cycle line under Today kept the old sprint length when you changed the length after picking the number of sprints.
  • Fixed: a sprint whose time ran out while the PC was asleep counted as finished. It's now judged like one that ran out while FlowShield was closed: finished only if FlowShield was watching for at least half of it.
  • Fixed: quitting FlowShield during a Soft sprint left it saved as running, so it came back on the next launch. Quitting now ends a sprint exactly as the End button would.
  • Fixed: starting a sprint from the tray or the hotkey while a blocked app was open did nothing. FlowShield now opens and asks about the open apps.
  • Fixed: switching to an empty blocklist profile mid-sprint and back could close a blocked app without its warning.
  • Fixed: Delete everything could leave your settings on disk and not restart FlowShield.
  • Fixed: after a first install, the Activate in FlowShield button on the purchase page could do nothing because the link wasn't registered. FlowShield now registers it each time it starts.
  • A break inside your sleep window now says the nightly shield is still up, instead of saying blocked apps are allowed.

1.0.8 21 September 2026

  • Blocked apps are warned before they are closed: a notice naming the app, a few seconds to save, and a request to close itself before anything is forced. Hard kill mode still closes immediately, which is the point of it.
  • Starting a sprint tells you which blocked apps are already open, before the shield goes up.
  • A summary card after every sprint: minutes focused, distractions caught, momentum, and the streak.
  • Set an intention before a sprint, and it is there again when the sprint ends.
  • A daily focus goal, with one day off every seven that does not break a streak.
  • Export your journal and sessions as CSV or Markdown, for any date range.
  • Momentum explained in plain words, with a thirty-day chart of it.
  • Native notifications, a countdown tray icon, taskbar progress, and a tray you can switch off.
  • Each shield now says what it does and what it is best for.
  • "Distractions blocked" counts apps, not processes: closing one app that runs seven of them counts once.
  • Fixed: the first sprint of a streak showed no "Day 1" on its summary card.
  • You agree to the terms and privacy policy before anything runs, and the agreement is recorded.
  • New icons throughout, and the colours are consistent across the app and the site.
  • The site now shows real screenshots and a recording of the shield closing an app, plus a FAQ that says plainly what FlowShield does not do.

1.0.7 15 September 2026

  • A three-step first run welcomes new users and explains the three shields.
  • Custom sprint lengths: choose any duration from 5 to 240 minutes.
  • Per-app on/off switch: turn blocking on or off for each app without removing it from the list.
  • App picker with starter suggestions for students and gamers.
  • Start with Windows now starts FlowShield quietly in the tray.
  • Branded app and tray icons, including a distinct running-sprint tray state.
  • Friendly error dialog that never claims a sprint is still guarded unless the app can verify it.
  • Smaller window minimums and adaptive layouts for small screens.
  • Thank-you page after purchase that keeps the licence key visible and copyable.
  • Download guide, support page, changelog, and accessibility improvements across the site.
  • No developer-only text shows up on customer-facing surfaces.

1.0.6 15 September 2026

  • A sprint that was running when FlowShield closed or crashed carries on when it reopens.
  • Ending a sprint early gets harder as the shield gets stronger: a short grace period on Soft, a wait on Firm, a typed phrase on Sealed.
  • Only one copy of FlowShield runs at a time; opening it again brings the existing window forward.
  • flowshield:// links open the app, so the purchase page can hand over a licence key.
  • Uninstalling removes FlowShield's start-up entry and link registration.

1.0.5 14 September 2026

  • The app's look now matches the website.

1.0.4 14 September 2026

  • 7-day free trial, then a one-time $4.99 purchase. No subscription.

1.0.3 14 September 2026

  • An email address alone can no longer unlock a licence.
  • What the purchase page says while you pay now matches what happens.

1.0.1 12 September 2026

  • Fixes to the first release.

1.0.0 11 September 2026

The first release of FlowShield for Windows.

  • Three shield levels — Soft, Firm and Sealed — chosen per sprint.
  • Blocklist of distracting apps, with every process of an app closed together.
  • 15, 25, 45, 60 and 90-minute sprints.
  • Momentum score and a simple day streak.
  • One-line session journal, stored locally.
  • Sleep blocking schedules that close blocked apps during a nightly window.
  • Hard kill mode, making Soft close blocked apps immediately.
  • All data encrypted locally with Windows DPAPI; nothing is uploaded.

See the FlowShield home page for what the app does today.
