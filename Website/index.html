Sleep blocking Pro

Three levels of resistance

Pick how much you trust yourself today. FlowShield will hold you to it.

Soft

Shield II

Firm

Blocked apps are closed on sight and stay closed for the rest of the sprint. The blocklist is still editable.

Shield III · Pro

Sealed

Blocked apps close on sight and the blocklist locks for the rest of the sprint.
