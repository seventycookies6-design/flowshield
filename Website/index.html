What is FlowShield?

A day of school, homework, and gaming — with a shield on the hours you choose.

You pick how hard the shield pushes back for each sprint. Distracting apps you put on the blocklist are nudged or closed; finished sprints compound into momentum instead of all-or-nothing streak theater.

  1. 01
    Build a blocklist

    Choose Soft, Firm, or Sealed and a length. FlowShield holds that line for the timer.

  3. 03

What does a Shield do?

Three levels of resistance

Pick how much you trust yourself today. FlowShield will hold you to it — from a quiet notice to a locked blocklist.

Shield II · flagship for homework

Firm

After school, when Minecraft and Discord are one click away: Firm closes blocked apps on sight and keeps them closed for the rest of the sprint, while you can still edit the blocklist.

  • Closes launchers Steam, Epic, and Minecraft processes on the blocklist are closed when they appear.
  • Holds the line Blocked apps stay closed for the rest of the sprint.
  • Still editable You can adjust the blocklist mid-sprint if a study tool got caught.
Shield I

Soft

For lighter stretches — between classes, or when you need Discord open for a group project but still want a nudge when a blocked game launches.

  • Records the hit FlowShield notes the blocked app when it appears.
  • Shows a brief notice Inside FlowShield’s own window — the distracting app keeps running.
  • Low friction Use Soft when a full close would be too harsh for the moment.
Shield III

Sealed

For the late-night review when you do not trust yourself to “just check” Steam: blocked apps close on sight and the blocklist locks until the sprint ends.

  • Closes on sight Same hard close as Firm for everything on the list.
  • Locks the list You cannot add or remove blocked apps mid-sprint.
  • Ends with the sprint Ending the sprint early unlocks the list again.

Escalating shield levels

Start with a brief notice inside FlowShield while the distracting app keeps running. Step up to closing blocked apps. Or lock the blocklist for the sprint.

Momentum first

Finished sprints compound into momentum. Abandon one and it decays gradually, while a simple day streak stays secondary.

One-line session journal

Every sprint ends with one optional prompt: what moved? The answer is stored locally with that session.

Sleep blocking

Set a nightly window where the shield raises itself. The 1 a.m. version of you doesn't get a vote.

Hard kill mode

Make Soft behave like Firm: blocked processes close immediately instead of producing the brief notice.

Local by default

Your sessions, blocklists and journal never leave your PC. They are encrypted locally with Windows DPAPI.
