## Problems / what the next bench user needs

- **Guest automation/access is the blocker.** Establish that a minimal interactive task writes a file, then verify PS Direct remains usable across the named checkpoint restore, before spending another run on product behavior. The credential XML used was exactly `G:\HyperV\FlowShield-Lab\Scripts\lab-cred.xml`; its password was never inspected or printed. The supplied credential worked initially and failed or lost its session after reboot/restore. No alternate credential was guessed.
- **Healthy integrations do not prove the user desktop is ready.** Final Hyper-V status was `Running` / `Operating normally`, all six integration services `OK`, 4 GiB assigned, uptime approximately 5m33s. Explorer readiness remains unverified. See `final-vm-state.json`.
- **There are no PNG screenshots.** None of the guest capture actions completed. No host screen was captured and no screenshot was fabricated. The screenshot expectations for checks 1 and 4 remain outstanding.
- **The bridge is single-request only.** It was not used concurrently. A single 20-second guest readiness attempt through lab.ps1 failed with `You cannot call a method on a null-valued expression.` after its request timed out; the bridge's exception handler does not handle a missing WebException response. Most operations used direct Hyper-V / PS Direct instead. All authored UI/installer waits have deadlines; later PS Direct job probes also had a 45-second host deadline.
- **Settings are an envelope, not a raw settings object.** A top-level `{"AutoCheckUpdates":false}` alone is ignored by SettingsService.Load. The prepared OFF runner uses the supported base64 envelope. The first, unused PowerShell draft was corrected; no OFF installation actually ran.
- **Runners are retained for inspection, not claimed as validated.** `ui.py` contains planned actions for the five checks, but none reached a verified product assertion. Its explicit process environment points to the existing local `C:\feed` directory. No source fix or app workaround was attempted.
