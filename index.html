No gamified streak confetti. Just a shield that holds, and an honest record of the work.

Escalating shield levels

Start with a gentle nudge overlay. Step up to hard-close. Or seal the session so even you can't unlock it early.

Momentum, not streaks

Finished sprints compound into a momentum score. Abandon one and it decays — gradually, not catastrophically.

One-line session journal

Every sprint ends with a single prompt: what moved? Months later it's the only log of your actual output that you'll trust.

Sleep blocking Pro

Set a nightly window where the shield raises itself. The 1 a.m. version of you doesn't get a vote.

Hard kill mode Pro

Blocked processes are terminated the instant they spawn — no overlay to click past, no three-second grace window.

Local by default

Sessions, blocklists and journal entries live on your machine, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Nothing is uploaded.

Three levels of resistance

Pick how much you trust yourself today. FlowShield will hold you to it.

Shield I

Soft

A full-screen reminder slides over the distraction. Dismissible — it only costs you a moment of honesty.

Shield II

Firm

Blocked apps are closed on sight and stay closed for the rest of the sprint. The blocklist is still editable.

Shield III · Pro

Sealed

Closed on sight and the blocklist locks until the timer ends. The escape hatch is gone, which is the point.
